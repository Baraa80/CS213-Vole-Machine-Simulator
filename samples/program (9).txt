Second model:
=============


====================================================================================
|;Vole Machine Program.                                                            |
|;Purpose: -Load two numbers(11,10).                                               |
|          -Do OR operation for numbers.                                           |
|          -Display the result(10) on the screen.                                  |
|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|200A;Load immediate value [0A]HEX->[10]DEC into register 0.                       |
|210B;Load immediate value [0B]HEX->[11]DEC into register 1.                       |
|7201;OR register 0 and register 1 , Put result in register 2.                     |
|3200;Store register 2 into memory[00] (Screen output).                            |
|c000;Halt.                                                                        |
====================================================================================
explain result:-

  in 8 bites
            [0A]HEX OR [0B]HEX -> [00001010]BIN + [00001011]BIN = [00001011]BIN =>[0B]HEX =>[11]DEC
